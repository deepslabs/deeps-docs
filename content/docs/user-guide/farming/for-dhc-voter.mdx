---

DPS holders can delegate tokens to a DHC node and receive a proportional share of its staking rewards on [DHC dashboard](https://test-dhcs.deeps.fi/).

## Delegate to a DHC

1. Click **Vote Button** in the statistics section:

![Click Vote Button](/dhc-voter-vote.png)

2. Choose one DHC node:

![Select DHC](/dhc-voter-select.png)

<Callout title="Tip">
You can enter the DID quick filter DHC node at the green prompt box position.
</Callout>

3. enter the amount of DPS to delegate and submit the transaction:

![Input Submit](/dhc-voter-input-submit.png)

4. The activation of a pledge requires waiting for one epoch

![Wait for active](/dhc-voter-wait-active.png)

The following delegation limits apply:

* Maximum voters per DHC: **3,000**
* Minimum delegation per voter: **200 DPS**

Only DHCs selected on-chain as committee nodes earn rewards for an era. Voter rewards are proportional to their share of the node's total stake.

Here are some useful tips for you:

* **Standby**: The node is registered but is not currently providing DHC services.
* **Serving**: The node is active and can begin earning rewards from the next reward era.
* **Exiting**: The node is leaving the service. Avoid creating a new delegation to a node in this state.

## Change a Delegation

Click **Update** in the device list, enter the new delegation amount, and submit the on-chain transaction.

![Click Update](/dhc-voter-update.png)

Select **Unstake All** to remove the entire delegation from the node.

![UnStake All](/dhc-voter-unstake.png)

Delegation changes take effect in the next reward era. An increase can take up to approximately **8 hours** to become active. After reducing a delegation, wait **24 hours** and then claim the unlocked amount manually.

## Reward Distribution

At the end of each reward era, rewards are distributed automatically to the voter account in proportion to its share of the node's total stake.
