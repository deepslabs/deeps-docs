---
title: DHC Farming
description: How to participate in DHC Farming as an Owner or Voter
---

[DHC Farming](https://dhcs.deeps.fi/beta_testnet) supports two participant roles: **Owner** and **Voter**.

* An **Owner** operates a DHC node and manages its service lifecycle.
* A **Voter** delegates DPS to a DHC node to earn a share of its staking rewards.

Users can choose to be an Owner, a Voter, or both.
