---
title: For DHC Owner
description: How to manage a DHC node as an owner
---

## View Devices

After configuring the server, connect the device owner's wallet to the **DHC dashboard**. The dashboard displays devices associated with the connected account. The account must hold DPS to pay transaction fees. A newly registered device initially has the **Standby** status.

## Stake DPS

**Option 1.** Select **Launch** from the device menu. The dashboard calculates the additional DPS required to meet the service threshold.

**Option 2.** Select **Vote** and delegate the full 20,000 DPS required by the node.

**Option 3.** Select **Vote**, delegate the owner's minimum 10,000 DPS, and wait for other voters to fund the remaining requirement.

The following staking limits apply:

* Minimum owner stake: **10,000 DPS**
* Minimum total stake required to join the service: **20,000 DPS**

## Join the Service

After the device reaches the staking threshold, select **Join Service** from its menu. The device changes to the **Serving** status after it successfully joins the DHC network.

The device must run the **latest supported image version** before it can join the service.

## Review Rewards

If the node remains online and operates normally, rewards are distributed automatically at the end of each reward era. The dashboard's statistics section shows total rewards, while the device list shows rewards for each device.

A DHC reward era is **1,200 blocks**, or approximately **60 minutes**.

## Manage a Device

Users can perform the following administrative operations:

* **Launch**: Add enough owner stake to satisfy the device's minimum requirement.
* **Transfer Device**: Transfer ownership permissions and the associated owner stake to another account.
* **Bond Stake ID**: Bind the device DID to another SID and use the stake associated with that SID.
* **Join Service**: Add the device to the DHC network. This action is available after the device reaches the minimum stake and runs a supported image version.
* **Update Commission**: Change the owner's commission rate. A higher rate allocates a larger share of rewards to the owner.
* **Refuse New Voters**: Prevent new voters from delegating to the device.
* **Allow New Voters**: Allow new voters to delegate to the device.
* **Remove Device Voters**: Remove voters from the device.
* **Exit Service**: Begin a standard exit from the DHC network. This action is available only while the device is **Serving**.
* **Remove Device**: Remove the device immediately. If the device is **Serving** or **Exiting**, this is treated as a forced exit with the following consequences:
  1. A **1,000 DPS** penalty is deducted from the owner's stake.
  2. Delegated DPS is returned to voter accounts.
  3. The device is removed from the DHC device list.
  4. Rewards accrued during the current era are burned.

Changes made through **Launch**, **Update Commission**, and **Remove Device Voters** take effect in the next reward era.

## Stop the Service

To stop a node without incurring a forced-exit penalty:

1. Select **Exit Service**. The device changes to **Exiting** and continues operating during the exit period. Selecting **Remove Device** at this stage performs an immediate forced exit and incurs a penalty.
2. Wait up to **one day**, or 144 heartbeat cycles, for the standard exit to complete and for the device to enter **Standby**.
3. Select **Remove Device** to remove the standby device and reclaim its DPS. Removing a device in **Standby** does not incur a penalty.
4. Shut down the physical machine or cloud instance only after the on-chain removal is complete. Delete unused cloud resources to avoid further infrastructure charges.

## Penalties

Here are some important rules for the owner:

* **Offline penalty**: A device must submit a heartbeat every **10 minutes**. After more than **two missed heartbeat cycles**, the owner is charged **0.5 DPS** for each applicable cycle.
* **Forced-exit penalty**: Removing a device while it is **Serving** or **Exiting** charges the owner **1,000 DPS**.
* **Automatic exit**: The system automatically starts **Exit Service** after a device misses more than **10 heartbeat cycles**. A device in the **Exiting** state must continue submitting heartbeats until the exit completes.
