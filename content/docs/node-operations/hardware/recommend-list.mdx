<Callout type="error">
DHC nodes **MUST run on hardware that supports Intel® SGX2**.

[https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000058764/software/intel-security-products.html](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000058764/software/intel-security-products.html)
</Callout>

## Physical Machine

* CPU: 1 × Intel Xeon 4310
* Memory: 8 × 16 GB DDR4 ECC registered DIMMs
* Data disk: 1 × 1.92 TB SATA SSD
* System disk: 1 × 480 GB SATA SSD
* Network: 2 × integrated 1 Gbps RJ45 ports and 1 × independent 1 Gbps port
* Power: 2 × hot-swappable 550 W power supplies with 1+1 redundancy
* Expansion: 2 × PCIe Gen 4 x8 slots and 3 × PCIe Gen 4 x16 slots

## Cloud Services

* Microsoft Azure: DCsv3 or DCdsv3
* Alibaba Cloud: g7t, c7t, or r7t
