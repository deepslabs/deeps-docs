# Committee Asset Fee Calculation

The workflow calculates a periodic asset-management fee from a committee's BTC and BRC-20 holdings. It deducts the fee from the committee's remaining pledge and transfers it from the committee creator to the mining pallet account.

## 1. Convert Assets to Credits

BTC is converted to credits using the configured BTC exchange rate:

$$
C_{\mathrm{BTC}} = A_{\mathrm{BTC}} \times \frac{R_{\mathrm{BTC},1}}{R_{\mathrm{BTC},0}}
$$

Each BRC-20 asset is converted in the same way:

$$
C_i = A_i \times \frac{R_{i,1}}{R_{i,0}}
$$

The total credit value is:

$$
C_{\mathrm{total}} = C_{\mathrm{BTC}} + \sum_i C_i
$$

An asset is ignored when its exchange-rate denominator (`rate.0`) is zero.

For asset amounts greater than $2^{68}$, the implementation shifts the amount right by 64 bits before multiplying and scales the result back afterward. This reduces fixed-point overflow risk but discards some low-order precision.

## 2. Deduct Free Credits

The configured free-credit allowance is deducted from the total:

$$
C_{\mathrm{billable}} = \max(C_{\mathrm{total}} - C_{\mathrm{free}}, 0)
$$

No fee is charged when the total credits do not exceed `free credits`.

## 3. Calculate the Refresh-Interval Rate

`AssetFeeRatePerYear` is stored as parts per billion:

$$
R_{\mathrm{annual}} = \frac{\text{AssetFeeRatePerYear.deconstruct()}}{10^9}
$$

The implementation assumes one block every six seconds:

$$
B_{\mathrm{day}} = \frac{3600 \times 24}{6} = 14{,}400
$$

It converts the annual rate to an epoch rate and then scales that rate to the asset refresh interval. Ignoring intermediate integer truncation, this is equivalent to:

$$
R_{\mathrm{interval}} = R_{\mathrm{annual}} \times \frac{B_{\mathrm{refresh}}}{365 \times B_{\mathrm{day}}}
$$

where $B_{\mathrm{refresh}}$ is `assets_refresh_interval()`.

The actual implementation performs integer division while calculating `asset_fee_rate_per_epoch`, so its result can be slightly lower than the simplified formula.

## 4. Convert Credits to the Native-Token Fee

The configured charge rate is represented as:

$$
R_{\mathrm{native/USDT}} = \frac{\text{charge\_rate.0}}{\text{charge\_rate.1}}
$$

The calculated fee is:

$$
F = C_{\mathrm{billable}} \times R_{\mathrm{native/USDT}} \times R_{\mathrm{interval}}
$$

In code, the calculation is equivalent to:

```rust
fee = asset_fee_rate * native_usdt_rate * billable_credits
```

The implementation uses saturating `FixedU128` arithmetic for these multiplications.

## 5. Cap the Fee by the Committee Pledge

The amount actually collected cannot exceed the committee's remaining pledge:

$$
F_{\mathrm{real}} = \min(F, P_{\mathrm{committee}})
$$

`real_fee` is deducted from the committee pledge. The creator's currency lock is reduced by the same amount, and the tokens are transferred to the mining pallet account.

## Conditions That Skip Charging

The workflow returns without charging when:

- the annual asset fee rate is zero;
- the native-token/USDT charge rate is unavailable;
- the committee has no remaining pledge;
- the committee is stopped;
- the total asset credits do not exceed the free-credit allowance.

When the committee is stopped, it is also removed from `CommitteesNeedUpdateAssets`.

## Implementation Note

When the calculated `fee` exceeds the remaining pledge, the transfer is capped at `real_fee`. However, each committee member's `IdentityAssetFeeRewards` value is increased by the uncapped `fee`. This difference should be reviewed to confirm whether rewards are intended to use `fee` or `real_fee`.
